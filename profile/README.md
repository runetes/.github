### Lessons from building & running platforms at scale

*A collective of platform engineers who built and operated a multi-tenant internal developer platform powering some of Europe's largest online marketplaces — now sharing what we learned along the way.*

## 👋 Who we are

We're a team of engineers who spent years designing, building, and operating a shared **internal developer platform** — a multi-tenant Kubernetes offering that let dozens of product teams build and ship with **speed, security, and scale**.

Behind the scenes, that platform powered marketplaces serving **millions of users every day**:

<div align="center">

🛒 **Leboncoin** (France) &nbsp;·&nbsp; 📦 **Kleinanzeigen** (Germany) &nbsp;·&nbsp; 🇮🇹 **Subito** (Italy) &nbsp;·&nbsp; 🏠 **Milanuncios** (Spain)

</div>

Running one platform across many countries, brands, and workloads taught us a lot about **building platforms as products**, **managing tenancy and isolation**, and **supporting genuinely diverse needs** from a single foundation. Runetes is where we write it all down.

## 🧭 Themes we care about

| | |
|---|---|
| 🏢 **Platform as a Product** | Treating internal teams as customers — paved roads, golden paths, and knowing when *not* to build. |
| 🧩 **Multi-Tenancy & Isolation** | Sharing infrastructure safely across teams, brands, and countries without blast radius surprises. |
| ⚙️ **Operating Kubernetes at Scale** | Fleets of clusters across regions and accounts — upgrades, autoscaling, and day-2 reality. |
| 🛡️ **Reliability & Incidents** | Post-mortems written honestly, so the next team doesn't relearn them the hard way. |
| 💸 **Efficiency & Cost** | Getting more out of every node — right-sizing, ARM, and doing the hard optimization once for everyone. |
| 🏗️ **Infrastructure as Code** | Managing large fleets declaratively, and the migrations that get you there. |
